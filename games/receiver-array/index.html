02 / FIELD NOTES

      
How acoustic telemetry works

      
Acoustic transmitter
A small tag on an animal that emits uniquely coded underwater sound signals.

      
Acoustic receiver
A stationary listening station that records a tagged animal when its signal can be heard.

      
Why the placement matters
Good spacing follows likely travel routes while avoiding large gaps.

      
Environmental effects
Noisy surroundings or vegetation can weaken signal range, while solid terrain can block it.

      
Model simplification
This training model uses simplified fixed effects. Real telemetry depends on many additional factors.
