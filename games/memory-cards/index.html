02 / FIELD NOTES

      
How the model decides

      
Mass and anatomy
Internal tags must remain within the teaching burden threshold and anatomical limits.

      
Research requirements
Battery duration, output class, sensors and attachment method can rule out a tag.

      
Best-fit rule
Choose the smallest and lightest available transmitter that passes all checks.

      
Animal welfare
Some individuals should not be tagged with the available equipment.

How to play

      

        
01
1. Read the individual
Length is context, but the matching calculation uses body mass and anatomy.

        
02
2. Read the mission
A mission may require battery duration, sensors, output and attachment type.

        
03
3. Test the smallest tag first
Reject tags that fail a check, then choose the smallest remaining option.

        
04
4. Choose no tag when needed
Too small to tag is correct when every available transmitter fails.

      

      

        
200 g fish · 3.6 g tag

        3.6 ÷ 200 × 100 = 1.8%

        0.9 ÷ 30 × 100 = 3%
      

      
    

  

  
    

      
About the science

      

        
Simplified decision model
The game uses current transmitter specifications and a configurable teaching threshold.

        
Burden calculation
Internal tag burden = tag mass in air ÷ fish body mass × 100.
